The Results Tab contains a spreadsheet-like table of key economic values, by age, for the selected regime,

Values Reported

The table contains the following columns (left to right):

  • Years Since Disturbance (yr) - even when reporting a height-based regime, the age will be displayed here as it is needed for economic calculations.

  • Top Ht. (m) - The average height of the 100 trees/ha of largest diameter at breast height.

  • Standing Merch Vol. (m³/ha) - Standing (live) Merchantable Volume (12.5+ cm utilization limit).

  • Commercial Thin Revenue ($/ha) - The sum of all Harvesting values except Final Harvest.

  • Final Harvest Revenue ($/ha) - Final Harvest Value.

  • Silviculture Activity Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Silviculture activities1

  • Final Harvest Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Harvesting activities1.

  • Commercial Thin Harvest Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Commercial Thin activities2

  • Road and Infrastructure Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Roads and Infrastructure activities1.

  • Annual Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Annual Costs activities1.

  • Manufacturing Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all Manufacturing activities1

  • Discounted Benefits ($/ha) - The sum of all discounted benefits.

  • Discounted Costs ($/ha) - The sum of all discounted costs.

  • NPV ($/ha) - Net Present Value (NPV) = The sum of discounted revenues and costs incurred over a single rotation.

  • SV ($/ha) - Site Value (SV) = The sum of discounted revenues and costs from an infinite series of rotations of the same length. Also known as soil rent, bare land value and soil expectation values.

  • IRR (%) - Internal Rate of Return (IRR).  ("n/c" means no convergence, i.e., no estimate)

  • B/C - Benefit/Cost Ratio = Discounted Benefits divided by Discounted Costs

1 Activities are grouped into sections reflecting the Costs Tab categories.

2 Commercial Thin Costs, contains activities from the Commercial Thin Harvest Additives section and the Harvest section.
