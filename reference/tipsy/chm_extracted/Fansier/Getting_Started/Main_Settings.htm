The central Main Settings area on FAN$IER's Main Screen is between the Tool Bar and Tabs row.  It contains the Discount Assumptions settings plus the features below:

Regime

From the drop-down pick-list, select a Regime to review and/or edit from the list of Regimes in the current project.  This selection is be referred to as the "current Regime" or "selected Regime".

Key concepts:  FAN$IER's Main Screen displays all settings and analysis results for one Regime at a time in order to review and/or edit them.  The Compare Tab is the one exception to this one Regime rule.  Analysis results are instantly updated when changes are made to individual settings.

Products

From the drop-down pick-list, select a Product for the current Regime.  Economic analyses are then based on the value(s) of this Product, as specified in the Benefits Tab.

The Product list is determined by the suite of products contained in the product-based yields obtained from the growth and yield model.  TIPSY and TASS III generate a similar suite of products.  FAN$IER's default selections from this suite are logs on the Coast, and lumber in the Interior.

Harvest Age

From the drop-down pick-list, select a Harvest Age (rotation length) definition for the current Regime.  The first four (4) entries in the list are rotation length definitions based on physical and economic criteria:

  • MAX MAI (12.5):  Culmination age of mean annual increment (MAI, merch volume 12.5+ cm), the definition of physical rotation.

  • MAX NPV:  Culmination age of net present value (NPV), the most common definition of economic rotation for a single regime.

  • MAX SV:  Culmination age of site value (SV), a better definition of economic rotation when comparing regimes with different rotation lengths (culmination ages).

  • MAX IRR:  Culmination age of internal rate of return (IRR), a alternate definition of economic rotation.

  • the remaining entries in the list represent the available age (or height) steps from the product-based yield data.  Note, product-based yield data is usually reported by age, but FAN$IER can also accommodate data reported by height.

NOTE regarding fertilization:  Harvest ages less than about 8yrs after fertilization are not recommended, because linear response estimates from TIPSY (etc) tend to over-estimate early response.

Send to Compare

Sends the current Regime, as currently configured, to the Compare Tab.

Discount Assumptions

Short Regime Summary

Displayed directly below Discount Assumptions is a short regime summary provided by the growth and yield model.  This information is for reference only, it cannot be edited in FAN$IER.   If a short version is unavailable, FAN$IER will display whatever summary information is present.   Complete (long version) summary information is displayed in the Regime Tab.
