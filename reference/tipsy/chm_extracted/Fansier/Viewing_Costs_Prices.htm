Viewing Costs & Prices

The Activity Costs & Product Prices viewer displays FAN$IER's current cost functions, plus cost and price defaults.  Documentation for selected cost defaults is also provided here.  Documentation for price defaults can be found under Products and Prices and Economics Background.

The Activity Costs & Product Prices viewer can be accessed in two ways:

  1. through the Help Menu within FAN$IER's Main Menu, and
  2. by clinking the icon associated with each activity in the Cost Tab.

 

Note:  Costs and prices cannot be modified within the viewer.  They can only be changed within the Cost Tab and Benefits Tab.
