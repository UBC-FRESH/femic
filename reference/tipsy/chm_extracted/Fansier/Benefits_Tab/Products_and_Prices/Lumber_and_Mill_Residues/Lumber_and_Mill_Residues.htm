TIPSY and TASS III produce different types of lumber estimates.  Lumber from TIPSY is graded; TASS III's is not.   Lumber estimates from other growth and yield models will likely be different, as well.

TASS III Lumber

TASS III provides one type of lumber estimate to FAN$IER: Ungraded.

TASS III produces lumber estimates itself using log dimensions generated by the SmartAxe bucking simulator incorporated in TASS III.  Total lumber volume per log in thousand board feet (mbf) is predicted based on log size.   Total lumber volume per log is then partitioned by dimension (i.e. 2x4, 2x6, 2x8, and 2x10) based on log size.  Resulting lumber volumes are reported ungraded, by dimension, on a per hectare basis.  FAN$IER uses default prices for #2-grade for each dimension. Contact User Support for more information.

TIPSY Lumber

TIPSY (version 4.3) provides three (3) different lumber estimates to FAN$IER.  TIPSY lumber estimates come from TASS II runs linked to bucking and sawmill simulators (SMARTAXE and SAWSIM) for trees of merchantable size (coast=17.5cm+; interior=12.5cm+).  Per hectare lumber volume estimates are generated in thousand board feet (mbf)1 by lumber grade  (i.e. SS, #1, #2, #3, #4) for four (4) structural lumber dimensions (i.e. 2x4, 2x6, 2x8, and 2x10).

TIPSY Pre-adj (pre-adjustment)

Lumber volumes (mbf/ha) for individual grades (i.e. SS, #1, #2, #3 and #4), which have not been adjusted for juvenile wood content.

Return to Top

TIPSY #2 & Better

Lumber volumes (mbf/ha) for grades SS, #1, and #2 combined, which have not been adjusted for juvenile wood content.  Grades #3 and #4 are not included.  FAN$IER uses default prices for #2-grade for this summation.

TIPSY JW-adj (juvenile wood adjusted)

Note:  This juvenile wood adjustment is considered tentative by the developers, and is recommended only for educational purposes.  This adjustment does not reflect National Lumber Grading Standards, which only consider juvenile wood indirectly through machine stress rating (MSR).

Pre-Adjustment lumber grades (above) are adjusted (degraded) for juvenile wood content based on the TASS-predicted percentage of juvenile wood in each board.    Lumber value adjustments are accomplished solely through lumber grade reductions (degrade) according to the table below.

TIPSY Mill Residues

Mill Residues are included with all three (3) lumber options and are reported in oven dry tonnes per hectare (odt/ha) in five (5) categories:  Chips, Sawdust, Shavings, Trims, Bark

Return to Top

TIPSY Lumber (earlier versions)

Lumber from earlier TIPSY versions (prior to 4.3) is a summation of grades, by dimension, mistakenly called "#2 & Better" since it included #3 and #4, as well.  FAN$IER applies the default prices for #2-grade to this sum.  Chips are the only mill residue reported.
