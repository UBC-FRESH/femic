Products and Prices

FAN$IER analyses are limited to the suite of product-based yields provided by the growth and yield model.  For information on using product-based yields from other growth and yield models, click here.

Once regime data from a growth and yield model have been loaded in FAN$IER, the associated product suite appears in the Products drop-down pick-list in the Main Settings area of FAN$IER's Main Screen.

FAN$IER contains default product prices for the product suite generated by both TIPSY ad TASS III.  Defaults prices represent multi-year averages adjusted to 2006 constant dollars.  The Benefits Tab provides two options for overriding default prices.
