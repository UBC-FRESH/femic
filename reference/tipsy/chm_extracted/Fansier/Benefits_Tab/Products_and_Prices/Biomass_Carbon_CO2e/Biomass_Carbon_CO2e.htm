TIPSY and TASS III provide the following three (3) forms of data to FAN$IER. Product suites from other growth and yield models will likely be different.

Biomass

Biomass in oven-dry tonnes per hectare (ODT/ha) is estimated for the above- and below-ground components (wood, bark, foliage, branches, roots) of live and dead trees (no decay) using individual-tree biomass equations developed by Ung et al. (2008).

Carbon

Carbon is not directly assigned value in FAN$IER.  Carbon is the intermediate step in calculating CO2e (below), carbon's economically-valued surrogate.  Carbon stock equivalents in oven-dry tonnes per hectare (ODT/ha) is calculated from the above biomass estimates using a conversion factor for temperate zones of 0.5g C/g (Matthews, 1993; Lamlom and Savidge, 2003) as follows:  Carbon stock = biomass * 0.5

Matthews, G. 1993. The carbon contents of trees. Forestry Commission, Surrey, UK.

Lamlom, S.H., and R.A. Savidge 2003. A reassessment of carbon content in wood: variation within and between 41 North American species. Biomass Bioenergy 25, 381-388.

CO2e

CO2e is calculated using the molecular conversion from tonnes of carbon to tonnes of CO2e, as follows:

CO2e (tonnes) = Carbon (tonnes) × 44/12

This estimate is “gross” tonnes before accounting for other carbon emissions and reductions from associated silviculture activities.  It is therefore the theoretical maximum CO2e stored.  To reflect the intent of CO2e storage, no harvesting or transportation costs are applied by FAN$IER.
