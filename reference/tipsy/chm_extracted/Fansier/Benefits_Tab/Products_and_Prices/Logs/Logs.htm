Logs

TIPSY and TASS III both provide log yields by grade to FAN$IER. Product suites from other growth and yield models will likely be different.

TIPSY uses merchantability limits of 17.5+cm DBH (coast) and 12.5+cm DBH (interior), less 30cm stumps and 10cm tops.  TASS III uses 12.5+cm DBH for both.

TIPSY and TASS III log yields are obtained from the SmartAxe bucking simulator linked to TASS.  Merchantable boles are bucked to length (8ft minimum) to maximize value, based on FAN$IER's default log prices.  Logs are then scaled and graded according to ministry standard grading rules (Section 6 of the British Columbia Ministry of Forests and Range Revenue Branch Scaling Manual).
