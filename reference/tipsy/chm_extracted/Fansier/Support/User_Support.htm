User Support

The Stand Development Modelling Group within the
Forest Analysis and Inventory Branch Branch of the
British Columbia Ministry of Forests, Lands, and Natural Resource Operations,
conducts and coordinates FAN$IER research and development activities, provides user support, and advises the Minister, Executive, and staff on growth and yield issues.

Please help us assist you by documenting the following steps before contacting user support:

  1. Briefly document what you were attempting to accomplish and the problems you encountered.

  2. If appropriate, document the steps you took so that we can reproduce the problem, and attach a copy of your FAN$IER file(s),

  3. Email this documentation to the user support contact below.

We also welcome suggestions for improving FAN$IER in the future.

The primary contact for user support and feedback is:
