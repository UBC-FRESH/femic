Getting Topic-specific Help

There are several options for accessing context-sensitive Help throughout the program.  Each of these options locates Help relevant to your current location in FAN$IER.

  • The Help Command in the Main Menu.

  • The Help icon on the Tool Bar.

  • The F1 key on your keyboard.

  • Question-marks in the upper right-hand corner of dialog boxes.

  • Note:  Question-mark boxes associated with individual Cost Tab activities are used to access the Activity Costs & Product Prices viewer.

Navigating within Help

Select Help Topics from the Help menu. Then select one of three methods to find the information you need.

  1. The Contents tab contains a series of folders organized by subject matter, much like chapters in a book. Click the folders to open them and view the folders and/or pages within.   Browse this Table of Contents for the information you need.

  2. The Index tab contains subjects and keywords.  Browse the list, or type a keyword in the search box at the top.

  3. The Search tab allows you to perform a full-text search by simply typing in the word or phrase you are looking for.  A list of topics will appear that include the word or phrase. Select one of these topics and the word or phrase you are looking for will be highlighted throughout in the text.

Other Navigation Aids

  • A yellow "breadcrumb" line appears at the top of every Topic page indicating your current location in the Help hierarchy.  You can use it to navigate upwards in the hierarchy.

  • Just below the Topic title of longer pages like this one, a yellow table of contents follows the words "In this Topic".  This lists all the subsections on the page and allows you to jump to each one.  The Hide/Show link toggles the list on and off.

  • Hotlinks to other HELP pages and websites are in blue font and underlined.  Website links will open in your web browser.

  • Back to Top links appear throughout longer Topic pages.

  • Use the Back button at the top of the Help window to return to the previous topic or the Forward button to retrace your path in the opposite direction.

  • Use the Hide button at the top of the Help window to hide the navigation panel on the left, and use the Show button to restore it.

  • Use the Print button at the top of the Help window to sent the displayed topic to your printer.
