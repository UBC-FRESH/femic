Regime File Header (from Yield Model)

The top portion of the Regime Tab displays the full regime summary provided by the growth and yield model.  This information is for reference only, it cannot be edited in FAN$IER.  A short version is displayed in the Main Settings area, below Discount Assumptions.

Age at base year

This is the age (default zero) to which all benefits and costs are discounted prior to calculating NPV, SV, etc.  All costs and benefits accrued before this age are considered sunk costs and ignored.   Age in FAN$IER is assumed to be years since disturbance, not tree age.

3 Geographic Variables

The following three variables are used to determine several of the default costs displayed in the Cost Tab.  They are normally set within TIPSY and TASS III then automatically passed to FAN$IER in the regime file.  However, these "from Regime File" assignments can also be overridden using the drop-down lists provided.

  • Forest District

  • Biogeoclimatic Zone (BEC)

  • Slope

Description Box

Space is provide at the bottom of the Regime Tab for user notes, e.g., documenting growth and yield and economic modelling strategies for the regime.  This information is saved in the FAN$IER (.FNS) file for future reference.  It is also reproduced at the top of the Compare Tab for reporting purposes.
