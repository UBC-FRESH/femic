OAFs: Combining

To calculate the combined OAF at any age, use this equation:

OAF1 * (1 - (1 - OAF2) * age / 100)

Here is an example of how OAFs are combined in TIPSY when both OAFs are set to 0.90 (10% reduction).

In this example, the OAF2 reduction grows by 0.001 per year (1.00-0.90 / 100 years) or 0.01 per decade, reaching the 0.90 specified at age 100.

Note:  OAF2 is not implemented until merchantable volume (12.5+) is available. Consequently, it's not unusual to see an OAF of 1.00 at age 10.

Notice also that OAF1 and OAF2 are applied sequentially.  If, for example, OAF1 is 0.85 and OAF2 is 0.75, then the combined OAF is 0.64 at 100 years.

Combined OAF = 0.85 x 0.75 = 0.64
