Jobs: Pruning

NOTE:  TIPSY does not support pruning treatments, but the user may include their own employment-related data if desired.   Pruning treatments are supported by TASS.

Jobs required to:

remove the lower branches to promote the formation of knot-free wood and lumber of high value.

Options:

User-specified: Your own local data

Default: 8.25 PD/ha at stand age 10 - district average. This is the same for all districts (provincial average) and lifts.

Function:

JOBS = (#Trees pruned/ha, #Trees pruned/hr, Hours worked/day)

JOBS = T1 / (T2 * H)

where:

JOBS = Average number of person days per hectare

T1 = Number of trees pruned/ha (default - all trees to a maximum of 2000/ha)

T2 = Number of trees pruned/hr (defaults - Lift 1: 19, Lift 2: 24, Lift 3: 22)

H = Number of hours in a day (default 7 hours/day)

Source:

Mike Stone (1992)

Comments:

  • Each lift is applied at the age you specify. The age, however, doesn't affect the number of jobs.

  • OAF’s and pre-commercial thinning can affect the number of trees available for treatment and the creation of jobs.

  • TIPSY won't allow you to have more jobs/ha than possible for the number of trees alive at the time of pruning.

See also Pruning.
