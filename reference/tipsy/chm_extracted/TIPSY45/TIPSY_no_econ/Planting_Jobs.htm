Jobs: Planting

Jobs required to:

establish stands artificially.

Options:

User-specified: Your own local data

Default: 2.0 PD/ha district average. This is the same for all districts (provincial average).

Function:

JOBS = f (Trees planted, Slope)

JOBS = 1.230597 + (0.000271 * T) + (0.021875 * S)

where:

JOBS = Average number of person days/ha

T = Number of trees planted/ha (i.e. the initial density specified in TIPSY before the application of OAF 1. See comment below.)

S = Average slope (%) of ground (default = 10%)

Source:

Employment statistics reported by Forest Practices Branch (FPB)

and Mike Stone (1992).

Comments:

  • Planting follows the regeneration delay if applicable.

  • OAF 1, if less than 1.0, reduces the number of trees established to account for areas that do not regenerate adequately. But in terms of employment, TIPSY assumes the entire area is planted (i.e., T = pre-OAF 1 initial density).

  • OAF 2 is irrelevant as it is always 1.0 at germination and will be negligible at the time of planting.

See also OAFs and Planting Costs.
