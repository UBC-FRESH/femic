Armillaria OAF: How it's applied

The Armillaria OAF has two distinct components within TIPSY:  mortality and volume loss of the remaining trees. The combined effect is displayed on the Stand Description and in the OAF Impact variable within the Custom table.

The mortality component is applied evenly for a given age across all diameter classes, and trees are moved to the Mortality table.

The volume component reduces total and merchantable volumes, as well as the log and lumber volumes, by a constant factor at a given age.

Removing volume from the stand implies that individual trees are becoming thinner, as the height growth of the stand is unaffected.  When Armillaria affects a healthy stand and volume decreases, TIPSY shifts trees to smaller diameter classes. A simple model of the relationship between diameter and volume was created using a cone, which approximates the shape of a tree.

The equation for a cone (V = r2h / 3) is used to determine the decrease in diameter due to volume loss. Volume loss for a given age is constant across all diameter classes. For each diameter class, a diameter value d is calculated (with the conical equation) using the unadjusted volume. The adjusted volume is then used to calculate an adjusted diameter value d’. Then, d and d’ are used to calculate a class shift parameter (d - d’). This indicates how the trees in a particular class have decreased in size.

For example, consider a stand where, at a height of 12m, there are 576 trees at the diameter class of 15cm with a combined volume of 36m/ha. Assume a 10% volume loss due to Armillaria. d is calculated as 14.105, d’ is 13.381, and d - d’ 0.724. This indicates a downward shift of .724 cm of the mean diameter for the trees in this diameter class.

Thus, trees on TIPSY’s stand table are moved down from higher diameter classes. The stock and mortality tables are then adjusted to be consistent with the new stand values.

In this example, the combined OAF (or overall effect on your stand) is calculated by taking 50% of the Fdi OAF and 50% of the Pli OAF. Above at age 100, OAF 1 is 0.50, OAF 2 is 0.80, and the effect of a High Armillaria OAF is 0.37. The total OAF for Fdi is 0.15, for Pli is 0.40, and the combined OAF is 0.28.

OAF for Fdi = 0.50 x 0.80 x 0.37 = 0.15

OAF for Pli = 0.50 x 0.80 = 0.40

Combined OAF = (0.15 x 0.5) + (0.40 x 0.5) = 0.28

Note: The apparent impact of Armillaria on the lumber recovery factor and on average revenue is not real. It is an artifact that occurs when an agent alters the distribution of trees in the diameter classes without affecting the log and lumber tables as one would expect. Consequently, log and lumber volumes will be slightly high, and economic analyses will understate losses accordingly. In effect, we’ve added a model (i.e., Armillaria) to TIPSY that can't be applied consistently to every dependent variable and table.
