Snag Specifications

The Snag Specifications dialogue box appears when a Snag Table is first requested.  Subsequently, it can be accessed with the Pencil icon on the Toolbar.  This dialogue box shows the underlying model, an example of the values calculated, and options for changing the model coefficients.

The underlying model is displayed under Snag Probability Model. The model has 3 independent variables:
(1) average years since death;
(2) average DBH; and
(3) interaction between the years since death and DBH.

The formula calculates the expected probabilities, which TIPSY then applies to its underlying mortality death or destruction of forest trees as a result of competition, disease, insect damage, drought, wind, fire and other factors (excluding harvesting) database. This determines the number of standing dead trees in a diameter class at a specific age.

Model coefficients are found under Species Coefficients. To change the coefficients of the snag model, select the species and then overwrite the default values shown.

To help understand the model, an example of the probability of a tree standing given years since death and a DBH appears in the box labelled Example. You can change the variables YearsDead and DBH to investigate the model at different times and sizes. Once you've changed either the species coefficients or the base YearsDead and DBH, click on the Probability? button to view the results.

NOTE: TIPSY's snag and coarse woody debris calculations do not affect biomass and carbon output.
