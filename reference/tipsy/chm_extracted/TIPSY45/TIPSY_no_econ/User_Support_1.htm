User Support

The Stand Development Modelling Group, within the
Forest Analysis and Inventory Branch of the
British Columbia Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development,
conducts and coordinates TIPSY research and development activities, provides user support, and advises the Minister, Executive, and staff on growth and yield issues.

Please help us assist you by documenting the following steps before contacting user support:

  1. Briefly document what you are attempting to accomplish and the problems you encounter.

  2. If appropriate, also document the steps you took in TIPSY and attach a copy of your TIPSY input file (.tip)so that we can reproduce the problem.

  3. Email this documentation to the TIPSY user support contact below.

We also welcome suggestions for improving TIPSY in the future.
