Broadleaf species

TIPSY supports two broadleaf species: red alder and trembling aspen.

TIPSY's database includes natural and planted yields for both species.  Planted yields were derived from natural yields.  Minor differences in height-age relationships result from incorporating seedling height (default 21 cm).

TIPSY applies pre-defined Age Decline Custom OAFs to both species by default.
