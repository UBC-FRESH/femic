Biomass and Carbon

Biomass and Carbon are reported in Custom Tables, which is accessed through the Tables Menu or the Tool Bar icon above.

Variables Reported

Within Custom Tables, the Sets of Variables drop-down pick-list contains separate variable groupings for carbon and biomass, each contains a similar list of 14 variables reported in oven dry units (tonnes/ha):

  • Live tree (biomass, carbon) stocks:  wood, bark, foliar, branch, above, roots and total.

  • Dead tree (biomass, carbon)  stocks change (no decay):  wood, bark, foliar, branch, above, roots and total.

NOTE: Dead tree values represent 'periodic recruitment', i.e., the change the dead tree stocks, due to mortality, occurring between the yield table steps (i.e. age or height), with no adjustments for decay.  Dead tree values are derived from TIPSY Mortality Tables only.   Snag and coarse woody debris values are not involved.

Background

See Biomass and Carbon: Models for information on the models behind the values reported by TIPSY.

The calculation of carbon biomass is critical for reporting carbon stocks and stock changes to explore future forest management and land-use policy alternatives (Kurz, et al. 2009).  The monitoring and reporting of carbon stocks and stock changes are also considered in international agreements such as the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFNCCC), the Kyoto Protocol, and the Montreal  Process.

Refer to the BC Forest Science Program extension note about carbon in the forests.
