The Model

The model behind the Armillaria OAF is based on the paper Armillaria mortality and growth loss estimates for Interior Douglas-fir in the ICH ecosystem (unpublished CFS report) which describes two components of stand loss.  The first is mortality, which also creates a related drop in volume, at any given age.  The second is volume loss of the remaining trees due to chronic non-lethal infection.  This paper outlines stand losses as follows:

Assumptions

  • A cone can be used to approximate the shape of a tree to derive a relationship between volume and diameter loss. This relationship is constant across all diameter classes.

  • Mortality loss occurs evenly across all diameter classes.

  • Armillaria can be applied to individual species (specifically, interior Douglas fir) in multiple species stands without compromising the overall stand model.

Limitations

  • Little is currently known about potential disease interactions with genetic improvement. To date, genetic selections for growth have not included disease resistance. Faster growing trees appear to contact larger amounts of inoculum sooner.

  • TIPSY does not account for natural in-fill following mortality. However, any effects related to late-rotation mortality in particular should be minimal.

  • Some effects of Armillaria may already be incorporated in TIPSY through data sources used to calibrate TASS. This is thought to be minimal given that TASS calibration techniques selectively emphasize potential growth.
