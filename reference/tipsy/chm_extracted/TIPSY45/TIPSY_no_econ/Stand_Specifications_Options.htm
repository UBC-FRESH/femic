This is one of four main dialogue boxes used to edit the settings required to generating output tables for a unique stand.   It is accessed through the main Edit Menu and the icon on the Tool Bar.

Settings in this dialogue box are divided (boxed) into three (3) main groups:

Stand Regeneration settings

Stand Regeneration (method)

Select one of the following three (3) stand regeneration methods to be applied to all selected species.  Each method has a unique temporal-spatial tree distribution pattern.

Clumped (natural regen)

Clumped has a tree spatial distribution pattern representing Clumped natural refers to trees or a stand that regenerated naturally from seed after disturbance, e.g., fire or logging regeneration.  It is only available for Hwc, At, Fdi, Pli, and Sw.   The tree temporal distribution pattern is the same as Natural.  NOTE:  Height Repression effects at high initial Density (>13,825) are only available with the Natural regen method.

In TIPSY's Clumped spatial distribution pattern, trees are randomly distributed around randomly located clump centres, with the number of clumps/ha is equal to 10% of the initial Density value, up to a maximum of 1500 clumps/ha.   Trees are assigned randomly to clump centers and are normally distributed about each centre with a standard deviation of 3 metres (1m for Hwc).  On average (pine), 68 percent will be within 3 metres of the plot centre, and 95% within 6 metres; very few (0.3%) will exceed 9 metres.   These clump parameters are based on field experience and data from young stands of lodgepole pine.  NOTE:  Although TASS can generate a wide range of Clumped patterns, database size constraints limit TIPSY to this one set of Clumped parameters linked to initial Density.

Natural (regen, random)

Natural has a totally random tree spatial distribution pattern representing non-clumped natural refers to trees or a stand that regenerated naturally from seed after disturbance, e.g., fire or logging regeneration.  NOTE:  Height Repression effects at high initial Density (>13,825) are only available with this regen method.

TIPSY's temporal distribution pattern (both Natural and Clumped) mimics annual germination trends over a period of 5-9 years (a function of Density), beginning immediately after the specified Regen Delay period.  The random (bell-shaped) temporal distribution pattern is reported as increasing tree counts, which peak at the end of the 5-9 yr establishment period.  However, this peak tree count is less than the specified initial Density due to previous mortality.    See Ages Definitions for more about temporal distribution patterns.

Back to Top

Planted

Planted is a square spatial pattern with minor variations reflecting operational planting irregularities. All trees are planted (established) in the same year.  The Planted option is not available for aspen or red alder.  Planted trees are automatically assigned default values for Stock Height (species dependent) and Genetic Worth (0%).  Both can be viewed and edited in Species Specifications.

Planted trees are positioned on a theoretical square grid pattern with a standard deviation of 10 percent of the inter-tree distance allowed along each axis. That is, trees in a plantation with 2-metre spacing (2500/ha) would vary by 0.2 metres in either direction along the row, and also at right angles to the row. Theoretically, 95 percent of the trees will be within 2 standard deviations or 0.4 metres of the true location in any direction. Almost all trees (99.7%) will be within 0.6 metres.

NOTE:  TIPSY's future density estimates for Planted stands do not include natural ingress Trees that naturally seed into plantations over time. TIPSY does not account for this..   Although Natural or Clumped options may better reflect spatial patterns for plantations with significant natural ingress Trees that naturally seed into plantations over time. TIPSY does not account for this., genetic worth can only be applied with Planted.

Delay (regen)

Regeneration Delay (yrs) is the number of years from disturbance (e.g. harvesting or fire) to the beginning of the stand establishment period.

The default Delay for natural regeneration (Natural or Clumped) is 2 yrs.  This default represents the average time lag until a good seed year.

 For Planted, the default is 0 yrs.  This default assumes planting takes place the same year as the disturbance.

When using TIPSY yield tables as input for forest-level (e.g., timber supply) models that already incorporate regeneration delay, set TIPSY’s regeneration Delay to 0.

Density (initial)

The Density button opens the Initial Density dialogue box, which accepts input in the form of Density (trees/ha) or the equivalent average inter-tree Spacing (m) value.   Only one of these two values is required.

TIPSY supports a maximum initial Density of 10,000/ha for Natural (incl. Clumped)  stands (250,000 trees/ha for a few species) and 4444 trees/ha for Planted stands. The minimum initial Density is 69 trees/ha for most species.  See Density Ranges for more detailed information.

Note for lodgepole pine only, Density values >13,825 trees/ha automatically trigger TIPSY's height repression effects unless PCT is applied to avoid repression.  

The Initial Density dialogue box also contains a button labeled Calculate from Existing Stand Data.  This opens the Existing Stand Data dialogue box, which provides an alternate method for estimating initial Density for existing established stands.  NOTE:  this option is only available for single-species stands.

Operational Adjustment Factors (OAFs) settings

About OAFs

OAFs are Operational Adjustment Factors used by TIPSY to adjust (usually reduce) its yield predictions (volume, stocking, etc) to account for various operational effects that are not otherwise accounted for by TIPSY.

NOTES:  TIPSY OAFs are simple multipliers with a neutral (no adjustment) value of 1.0.  The standard ministry defaults, OAF1=0.85 & OAF2=0.95, represent "average" stand conditions for forest-level analyses (e.g., timber supply).  Adjustments for extra-ordinary or stand-specific conditions generally involve professional judgement supported by field data.   Users developing TIPSY yields for use in ministry applications (e.g., TSR, etc) are urged to consult application-specific OAF policy before deviating from the defaults.

While individual OAFs may be greater than 1.00, the combined (net) effect cannot increase yield.   Therefore, TIPSY caps the net effect at 1.00 at each age, and a message appears when this occurs.

Back to Top

OAF1

OAF1 is a constant multiplier applied uniformly at all ages.  The ministry default is 0.85.  OAF1 is used to reduce yields for various abiotic and biotic factors not accounted for by TIPSY.  Small, unmapped non-productive area (NP, e.g., rock and wetland) is normally the dominant abiotic factor, but it can also include weather-related stocking loss (e.g., wind, snow, ice).  Biotic factors include competition-related stocking gaps on otherwise productive ground, e.g., growing space occupied by non-commercial species or brush, and other endemic losses, e.g., forest health impacts, etc.

Back to Top

OAF2

OAF2 is a progressive multiplier; the value and impact increases with age.  The ministry default is 0.95.  The default is generally assumed to account for merchantable volume losses due to decay, waste, and breakage (DWB).  However, OAF2 can also be used for endemic biotic factors (e.g., forest health impacts) with yield effects that increase over time.

An OAF2 value is indexed to age 100, such that its specified value (e.g., 0.95) equals its yield impact at age 100.  The impact increases 1% per year reaching the specified value at age 100, and continues increasing by 1% per year thereafter. E.g., for an OAF2 of 0.90, yields are multiplied by 0.999 at age 1, 0.95 at age 50, 0.90 at age 100 (the index year), and 0.80 at age 200, etc.

Custom OAFs

Optional stand-level Custom OAFs can be assigned by checking the box next to the Custom OAFs button.  This opens the Custom OAFs Intensity dialogue box to configure Custom OAFs for the stand.  Unchecking the box disables these OAFs, but retains the current Custom OAF settings.

NOTE:  Species-specific Custom OAFs can be assigned in Species Specifications. TIPSY's OAFs for Armillaria root-rot (ICH Douglas-fir only) are available as Custom OAFs.

WARNING:   The combined effect of ALL OAFs is additive.  Therefore, whenever individual OAFs are added or modified, all existing OAFs must be re-evaluated and modified in context.

Variable Retention/Windthrow

Optional adjustments for Variable Retention (VR) and Windthrow impacts can be made by checking the box next to the Variable Retention/Windthrow button.  This opens the Variable Retention dialogue box for settings configuration.  Unchecking the box disables VR, but retains the current VR settings.

Treatment settings

PCT

Pre-commercial thinning (PCT) is activated by checking the box above the PCT button, which then displays the currently selected Residual Density (post-PCT, trees/ha).   To change residual density, click the button to open the Residual Density dialogue box.  Unchecking the box disables PCT, but retains the current PCT settings.

The Residual Density dialogue box also provides the option to estimate Residual Density from Existing Stand data post-PCT.

The timing of PCT is fixed within TIPSY at a height of 4 metres in the interior and 6 metres on the coast.

Avoiding lodgepole pine height repression with PCT:   When working with extremely dense stands of natural lodgepole pine subject to height repression (> 13,825 trees/ha initial Density), the Residual Density dialogue box displays the PCT prescription that avoids height repression.  Checking the associated box indicates the specified critical height threshold (PCT timing) will be met, although the user may select any residual density below the specified density threshold.   When the box is unchecked, TIPSY applies its repression effects model.

Select PCT pre-post data in the Preferences Menu to display pre- and post-PCT stand conditions within Yield Tables.

Back to Top

Comm. Thinning

Commercial Thinning (CT) is only available for single-species stands of coastal Douglas-fir and interior lodgepole pine, which also have sufficient density at time of thinning to accommodate TIPSY's minimum CT residual densities.   Otherwise, the check box remains inactive.

Checking the box opens the Commercial Thinning dialogue box enabling selection of Thin at: (entry timing) and the Residual Density of Stems >= 12.5cm.   Entry timing must be specified by (top) height (m); selection by age is not available at this time.   Unchecking the box disables CT, but retains the current CT settings.

Preference options control how CT yields are reported.

Fertilization

Fertilization involves settings in two different locations within Interactive TIPSY.   The settings here in Stand Specifications control application timing and effectiveness, while settings within Species Specifications determine which species-specific response models are applied.  I.e., Ministry default response models are applied by default unless custom user-defined response models are specified in Species Specifications.
