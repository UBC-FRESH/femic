Percentage of retention (i.e. 10, 20 and 30%) defines the total percentage of crown area retained as either aggregated groups or dispersed trees. These percentages are the same for all harvesting ages and site indices. The size of the TASS simulations ranged from 0.890 to 6.7 hectares, and the size of the aggregated groups ranged from 0.01 to 1.5 hectares. The number and sizes of the retained groups were assigned to match the corresponding retention percentages. This was necessary because in certain situations VR prescriptions specify only the number of groups and not their size. Then, for each retention percentage, the size of the simulated stand was adjusted accordingly to maintain the ratio between the size and number of groups. For instance, for 10% retention we could have:

9 groups of 0.01 ha (i.e. total area of 0.09 ha) in a TASS simulated area of 0.9 ha
or alternatively
1 group of 0.4 ha in a TASS simulated area of 4 ha

The selected aggregated group shape was rectangular with a width/length ratio of 1:6.25. This shape was subjectively selected after assessing the most common group shapes in actual VR blocks from the Chilliwack Forest District.

Preliminary VR simulations done by Goudie (1999b) demonstrate that the future growth and yield of the regenerated trees is directly affected by the pattern of retention. The number, size, and shape of the retained groups define the aggregated pattern. The dispersed pattern is defined by the number and distribution of individual trees.

Goudie (1999b)  concluded that the "edge length" is one of the key factors differentiating VR regimes because it numerically accounts for the pattern of the retained trees. It reflects the ability of the retained stand to occupy growing space over the subsequent rotation.

The aggregated edge length was calculated from TASS output at the time of VR harvest as follows:

Aggregated group area (ha) = (Width x Length) / 10000

Edge length per group (m) = 2(Width + Length)

Edge length per ha (m/ha) = (Edge length per group / Simulation area) x No. of groups

We assumed dispersed tree crowns were circles, thus edge length per ha was calculated as:

Crown edge length (m) = Average crown width x 3.141516

Edge length all trees (m) = Crown edge length x Number of retained trees

Edge length per ha (m/ha) = Edge length all trees / Simulation area

Regression Formulas and Coefficients

Variables

edge - Total edge (per hectare) of the retained overstorey

VRAF - VR adjustment factor for merchantable volume 12.5+ cm (i.e. the volume ratio between the regenerated VR volume and a clearcut volume)

perc - Crown cover percentage of the retained groups

topht - Top height of the regenerated stand

retht - Top height of the retained groups

SI - Site index

logp - Natural logarithmic conversion of average group size in hectares of either the aggregated groups or the average crown area of the dispersed trees.

Formulas

VR Adjustment:

VRAF= a + b * edge + c * (edge - x0) * p1

a = a0 + a1 * SI + a2 * perc

b = b0 + b1 * retht + b2 * perc + b3 * topht

c = c0 + c1 * SI + c2 * retht + c3 * perc

Condition:

p1=1 if edge > x0, else p1=0
