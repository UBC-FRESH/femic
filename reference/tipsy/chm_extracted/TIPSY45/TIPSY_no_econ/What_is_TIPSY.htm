What is TIPSY?

Click here to see What's New in this version.

The Table Interpolation Program for Stand Yields (TIPSY) is a growth and yield program that provides electronic access to managed stand yield tables generated by TASS and SYLVER. Check the Ministry's Growth and Yield Modelling Website for more information.

TIPSY retrieves data from its database of TASS-generated yield tables, customizes the information and displays summaries and graphics for a specific site, species and management regime.   Yield tables are currently available for eight pure even-aged coniferous species of commercial importance in British Columbia, and two broadleaf hardwoods, red alder and trembling aspen.  Yields for other species are derived through species substitution and applying species-specific site index equations.

An optional multiple species feature prorates the yields for up to a maximum of eight species in TIPSY, and five in BatchTIPSY. This option was developed to aggregate stands for the benefit of timber supply planners. TIPSY does not simulate the growth of multiple species stands biologically. The only biological assumption is the site index conversion adjustment among species.

TIPSY projects the growth of managed stands starting at age zero in support of silvicultural planning and timber supply analyses. New TASS III software is also available for simulating multi-cohort stands. Users should consider VDYP for even-aged, natural stands.

Treatments in TIPSY, which vary by species, include initial density, fertilization, spacing and commercial thinning. Special features include genetic improvement, repression, variable retention, windthrow, input of limited data from existing stands (i.e. height, number of trees, and treatment), operational adjustment factors (OAFs) , and an integrated batch processing mode. TIPSY generates the following information: standing yields, snags, coarse woody debris, products (e.g., logs, lumber, biomass and carbon equivalents), and employment summaries.  TIPSY can also pass yield data directly to PLOTSY Independent software packaged with TIPSY used to generate XY graphs of TIPSY data. for graphical analysis, FAN$IER for economic analysis, and CBM Carbon Budget Model: independent software developed by the Canadian Forest Service for conducting carbon budget modelling and analysis using growth and yield data from TIPSY (etc). for carbon budget analysis.

Reliability

The yield information in TIPSY was calibrated to conform to a database in excess of 15,000 permanent sample plots. The number of plots varies greatly by species, treatment, and site index. The following reliability estimates are based on the number of plots by species, experience, and basic knowledge of tree growth and stand development. Reliability also varies by treatment, stand age and other factors.

Guidelines for use

TIPSY supports a wide range of user input values.  Where appropriate, default values reflect ministry recommendations based on best available information (e.g., site index curves and fertilization response).   For example, western hemlock has a default fertilizer response of zero, but users can also enter their own response values for one of the few hemlock sites (e.g. CWHvm1/Salal/North Island) known to respond to fertilizer.   In any case, TIPSY input should be supported by professional experience, due diligence, knowledge of local conditions, and an appreciation of stand dynamics.

If TIPSY does not offer the silvicultural treatment or level needed, consider using TASS III, or contact User Support to request special customized runs with TASS II (e.g. pruning, ingress, and specific spatial distributions).
