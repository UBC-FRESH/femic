New features and updates include:

  • Database and computational changes:

    • Yield changes for Pli and Sw reflect new data and modelling techniques from TASS III, which have also been incorporated in TASS II.   Yield comparisons (old vs. new) are available for Download under TIPSY.  The Pli TIPSY database is now based on SI 23 and Sw uses SI 20.

    • Slight changes in Fdi yields due to revised Site Height and Top Height linkages.

    • Slight changes in Cw yields due to application of new SI curves.

    • Updated SINDEX.DLL to ver. 1.51, which includes new Cw SI curves, Nigh 2016.

  • DWB OAFs (Decay, Waste, Breakage) removed from Interactive and Batch.   The models, developed primarily with older age-class data from natural stands, are less appropriate for young, managed stands.   E.g., the models generated abrupt volume changes at the 120yr age-class boundary.

  • WS (well-spaced) stats removed from Custom Tables due to concerns that model-based estimates were unable to align with field methods.  

  • Help heavily revised and updated.

  • Minor bug fixes.

TIPSY Version 4.3 new features and updates:

  • Updated TIPSY database that includes:

    • new fire suppression variables such as: mean crown base height, mean crown length, and crown bulk density

    • clumped spatial distribution for white spruce

    • maximum inter-tree average spacing increased to 12 m from 6 m giving a minimum initial stand density of 69 trees/ha from 278 trees/ha in TIPSY 4.2

    • improved bucking and sawmilling practices in the interior and coastal regions for lumber and residual products

  • A completely new database for interior Douglas-fir (Pseudotsuga menziesii  var. glauca). A full TASS recalibration for the species was recently completed, based on new destructive sampling stem and crown data collected from the B.C. interior, and updated permanent sample plot calibration data sets from B.C. and the U.S. Pacific Northwest.  Users will notice some differences in the yield tables when comparing them with TIPSY 4.2 results. Volume projections are now slightly to significantly lower at mid to low initial stand densities, with the discrepancy increasing with tree spacing, age, and site index. At higher initial densities and site indices, volumes are slightly higher for older stands because of higher stem retention.  Furthermore, projections now extend to only 45 m top height, which marks the limit of our data for interior Douglas-fir.  For a volume comparison (4.2 vs 4.3), click here.

  • Updated TIPSY to CBM-CFS3 feature to link and transfer TIPSY managed stand yield tables to the Operational Scale Canadian Forest Service Carbon Budget Model version 1.2.4853.195.

  • Please note that some of the Help documentation topics may be incomplete due to Workforce Adjustment in May 2010.

Changes incorporated:

The last general release of TIPSY (version 3.2) contained some bugs.  We posted notices on the TIPSY web page and updated the downloadable version there as well, but didn't notify users.  A bug in the fertilization option became apparent in the fall of 2006.  The bug was significant enough to warrant this release of version 4.1.  Here's a brief description of that bug, plus others that were in version 3.2.

  • The species-specific fertilization response information in TIPSY is based on 5m steps of site index. If your pine stand has a site index of 18 and is 10m in height, TIPSY should interpolate between the response for site index 15 (11% increase) and the response for site index 20 (17% increase). For most species and site indices, there was no interpolation. As soon as TIPSY exceeded site index 15, the 17% increase kicked in.

  • TIPSY and BatchTIPSY weren't applying the fertilization competition adjustment correctly in thinned stands.

  • BatchTIPSY 3.2a and 3.2b substituted zeros for some missing variable retention values, if there were other variable retention values present in that row. BatchTIPSY 3.2d and later will not produce a table unless all specified columns in the input file contain variable retention data.

  • BatchTIPSY wasn't checking correctly for valid ranges for site indices.  BatchTIPSY 3.0 checked the site index for the first species only in each row of the user's input file, and allowed a range of 5 to 50m for that species. No other site indices in that row were checked for a valid range. BatchTIPSY 3.2 checked to see that the site index of the first species fell within the range for the site indices of all species in that row! For example, if the first site index was 7 m for pine, and you also had interior redcedar in that run, BatchTIPSY would output a table of zeros, since the lower limit for interior redcedar site index is 10, not 7.

  • While correcting version 3.2 of BatchTIPSY in January 2005, an error was inadvertently introduced. Under certain circumstances, BatchTIPSY checked incorrectly for site index ranges, didn't run, and produced an error message saying that the site index range for a particular species is 0 to 0.

  • In any version of TIPSY prior to 3.2b and BatchTIPSY prior to 3.2k, uninitialized variables in TIPSY and BatchTIPSY caused the programs to give different answers for the same inputs.

New features in version 4.1 include a windthrow module added to the variable retention feature, and a correction to aspen mortality.  Aspen now dies off completely before age 160 in all runs.

Changes incorporated:

  • Some species were previously unavailable in TIPSY because of lack of data. We recommended species substitutions in the Help. Now you are able to use site curves that were developed for that particular species, still substituting the volume-height data of a different species in TIPSY's database. When you do this, TIPSY also substitutes many default values of the species in its database. See the new list of species substitutions.

  • New juvenile height models for lodgepole pine and interior spruce. The lodgepole pine default site curves have been changed to Thrower (1994). You might also notice that some site curves have an '(ac)' following the developer's name. Read about this in Site Curve Modifications. Contact Gord Nigh for questions about site curves.

  • Variable retention adjustment factors.

  • Fertilization was enhanced to include multiple species, multiple applications and more response data.

  • The option to create your own custom OAFs, at both the stand and the species level.

  • More operational factors, including a decay, waste and breakage model that mimics VDYP's DWB model, and an OAF developed specifically for Armillaria in the ICH.

  • Multiple crop tree summaries through the custom table.

  • You now enter planting stock height, rather than age.

  • A new table combines yield and economic data, as well as height and age, for 'events' such as commercial thinning.

Changes incorporated:

  • Fertilization for single species stands

  • Existing stand, or startup, feature calculates initial density

  • Genetic gain for multiple species

  • The Batch TIPSY interface was written for MS Windows and incorporated into the TIPSY product. It now includes all features in TIPSY that have an effect on volume, such as genetic gain and fertilization.

  • Commercial thinning for coastal Douglas-fir and interior lodgepole pine

  • Clumped distributions for coastal hemlock and interior lodgepole pine

  • Coarse woody debris

  • Jobs module

  • Upgrades to the Plot Program for added flexibility. It now has its own online documentation, too, which makes it totally independent.

  • Custom, or tailor made, tables, with the addition of crop tree statistics. See Crop Trees to see how OAFs affect crop trees refers to a user-specified number of trees/ha (12.5+cm). The associated statistics (e.g., merch vol) are affected by OAFs. and prime trees differently.

  • Tables that mimic 'Timber Supply' and 'VDYP' output

  • TIPSY 3.0 uses the Nigh 1999 site curves for lodgepole pine, replacing the Goudie 1994 dry site curves. Merchantable volumes (12.5+) at stand heights of 12 to 30 m change by a noticeable amount in plantations and natural stands with the exception of low establishment densities (<1 000 trees/ha) where changes are relatively small. At the higher densities, the volume of natural stands tends to decline by 5 to 15 m3 (0 to 13%). Planted stands behave a little differently at these higher establishment densities. At heights of 12 to 20 m, volumes differ by about -15 to 15 m3 (-15 to 6%). At heights of 22 to 30m, volumes differ by about 15 to 30 m (3 to 6%). The maximum observed difference in the suite of test runs was 30 m3 (6%). Ninety percent were within 20 m3 (5%) and 50% were within 10m3 (4%).

Also, since version 2.1, we have had to rerun TASS and we used the Nigh 1999 site curves. This means that, even if you choose Goudie 1984 (dry site), you won't be able to reproduce the pine yields that version 2.1 produced.

Changes incorporated:

  • Lodgepole pine repression

  • Stand and stock tables, log table

  • Snag and mortality tables, and the capability to edit snag specifications

  • Economic summary, and the capability to edit economic specifications

  • Plot program plots negative values, plus all variables in the new output. Other enhancements include options for naming the axes and automatic scaling of axes to fit the data.

  • Since there were so many tables in TIPSY's output, we added a 'multiple view' feature. You can view a single table on the screen, or have many tables, or 'views', open at the same time. This option was provided for viewing convenience only (not printing, etc.)

  • Changed Operational Adjustment Factor from a percentage to a factor (0.00 to 1.00).

  • Incorporated Sitka spruce site curves (Nigh 1997). At low sites, yield decreased slightly, and at higher sites, yields increased slightly. As an example, at site index 20 the yield (merch vol 12.5+) is roughly 6% lower at age 100. At site index 35, the yield is roughly 4% higher.

  • The age used to calculate OAF 2 was changed to years since germination for both planted and natural stands at the request of Timber Supply Branch. See Ages Defined

  • Natural stands in WinTIPSY 1.3 and earlier had a germination period (seed year to stand establishment) of 2 years built into the system. In TIPSY 2.1, you had to include this period in the regeneration delay.

  • Planted stands in WinTIPSY 1.3 were automatically planted with 2 year old stock. In TIPSY 2.1, you can enter the age of the stock. TIPSY defaults to 1 year old stock.

  • Output is available in imperial units for the benefit of our U.S. clients. TIPSY can change from one to the other 'on the fly'. Of course, some things such as board feet don't change. And all TIPSY's documentation, online or printed, uses metric references and examples.

  • Yield table results (volume, stem count, etc.) are +/- 3% of earlier TIPSY runs. TIPSY's database is built by TASS. The TASS v2.05.24 executable was created on Dec. 17/92. Since that time, we have upgraded both TASS's operating system and compiler tools. We also modified the source code to include rings/2cm information and mortality summary output. This updated source code was compiled on October 9, 1997. Our only explanation for the differences is that the updated compiler fixed subtle inconsistencies or slightly altered the precision of floating point values.
