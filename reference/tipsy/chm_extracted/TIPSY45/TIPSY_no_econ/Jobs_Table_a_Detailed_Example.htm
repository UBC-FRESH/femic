Jobs: Understanding the Output

The following contrived TIPSY run illustrates many of the silviculture and harvesting options that create employment. Notice that silviculture jobs are calculated at the time of treatment and accumulated. Harvesting and manufacturing jobs are calculated at each entry and are not accumulated with the exception of jobs created by commercial thinning (CT). These jobs are determined in the year of CT and added to all future calculations of the number of jobs created by the final harvest. Potentially, the final harvest could occur in any year – even if there is no merchantable wood!

TIPSY estimates the number of harvesting and manufacturing jobs created at each age even though the information is rather meaningless until a reasonable harvest volume is available.

If a stand is to be commercially thinned in a particular year, TIPSY will calculate the number of jobs created under two different scenarios:

  • The stand is harvested before the scheduled entry thereby removing the CT opportunity.

  • The stand is commercially thinned - and then the residual stand is immediately harvested independently. This option is not realistic. The sum of the jobs created by both operations is reported but is of little interest except as noted in the next paragraph. The number of jobs attributed to CT is reported in the final line of the table along with the merchantable volume removed. If it doesn't appear in your table, check to see that you have the Display CT Values and CT pre/post data options selected.

It is important to note that very few of the person days (PD/ha) required for commercial thinning contribute to the creation of extra employment. This is because CT reduces the labour requirements of future harvests. To calculate the extra work, repeat the run without treatment and look at the difference in the PD/ha assigned to harvesting and manufacturing at a particular harvest age. This difference will vary a little over the range of potential harvest ages. In TIPSY, the extra jobs created by CT are calculated at the time of treatment as follows:

PD/ha required if the stand is not commercially thinned

- PD/ha required to CT the stand

+ PD/ha required to harvest the residual stand

This net PD/ha (extra CT work) is added to all future jobs reported for harvesting and manufacturing. Details are illustrated below.

In this example, the CT entry occurs at a ‘fractional’ age (64.8 years) because TIPSY thins the stand when it reaches a height of 33 m, and then determines the corresponding age. You can't specify the age at which the stand is to be thinned - only the height.

Note that this output was created with a previous version of TIPSY.
