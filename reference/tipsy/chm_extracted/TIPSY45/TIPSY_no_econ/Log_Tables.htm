About Log Tables

Log Output Tables are accessed through the Tables Menu or the Tool Bar icon above.   Log Tables report log volumes (m3/ha) by grade for the trees displayed in the stand and stock tables. Log volumes by grade can also be reported in Custom Tables.

TASS's bucking simulator bucks logs into lengths that maximize log value (based on FAN$IER default values).  Next, TASS's scaling and grading algorithms apply ministry standard grading rules (Section 6 of the Scaling Manual, BC Ministry of Forests and Range, Revenue Branch). The Log Table summarizes the total scaled volume and the grade breakdown. The total scaled volume departs somewhat from the merchantable volume in the yield table because the volumes are calculated differently.

  • The yield table gives the total volume of trees 12.5 cm and greater less tops (10 cm) and stumps (30 cm).

  • Log tables show the scaled volume of logs which are 2.5 m (8 ft) or longer in length. Trees in the 15 cm (or 20 cm) diameter class will have some "merchantable" volume but the distance between the stump and top may not be long enough to satisfy the minimum length requirement.

As a result, the total volume in the log table will fall short of that reported in the yield table by an amount that declines as the stand ages and the proportion of trees in the lowest diameter class decreases. The scaled volumes can exceed the volumes in the yield table because of scaling assumptions concerning taper, particularly butt swell.

H & I grade issues

The H grade requires a minimum 5 annual growth rings per cm, which causes problems for TIPSY. TIPSY cannot translate this information across the range of site indexes, so the distribution of volume between grades H and I may be incorrect. The distribution is only correct for site index 35 on the coast and site index 20 in the interior. The H grade will be underestimated at lower site indexes and overestimated at higher site indexes. Note that for cedar there is no error in the grades, since minimum growth rate does not apply to cedar grades.

Multiple Species

For multiple species stands, TIPSY will only display log grade volumes for all species combined. This is because TIPSY processes each species in the stand as a pure species, then prorates the volumes in each column according to the percent composition you have specified.

Red Alder

TIPSY's red alder log tables are based on 2.5 m sawlog length. Any log that doesn't meet the criteria is put in the pulp category. See Red alder in TIPSY for more information.
