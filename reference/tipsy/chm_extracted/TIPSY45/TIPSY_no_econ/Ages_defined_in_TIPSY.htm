Introduction

TIPSY currently defines and utilizes several types of ages, each with a unique definition and application.  These are the current age definitions used in TIPSY: 

Ages within Outputs

TIPSY Age:

(years since disturbance, disturbance age)  TIPSY Age and Top Height are automatically included in all TIPSY Outputs.  TIPSY Age reflects the time since the disturbance (e.g., fire, harvesting, etc) associated with stand establishment.  Disturbance age is commonly used in forest-level analysis (e.g., timber supply) applications.

TIPSY Age = regen Delay + Stand Age

                    = 0, represents the disturbance year.

BH Age:

(age at breast height, 1.3m)  BH Age and Top Height reflect the assigned Site Index.

For Planted stands:

Planting Age = Stand Age =  Establishment Age (no longer in TIPSY)

Total Age = Germination Age
                   = BH Age + years to breast height (YTBH)
                   = Planting Age + planting stock age (1)

Planting stock age is no longer a TIPSY input; it is has been replaced with Planting Stock Height.   TIPSY currently assumes a fixed planting stock age = 1.

For natural stands (Natural or Clumped):

Total Age = Stand Age = Germination Age = Establishment Age
                   = BH Age + years to breast height (YTBH)


NOTE:   Total Age, Planting Age, and BH Age can only be reported in Custom Tables, and they are not available for multi-species stands.

Ages as Inputs

TIPSY Age, Total Age, Stand Age, and BH Age (definitions above) are all input options within TIPSY's Age/Height dialogue box.

OAFs are referenced to Germination Age.  Timing of Fertilization is referenced to Stand Age.   Timing of Pre-commercial and Commercial Thinning are both referenced to Top Height, not age.

Note in the table above,

  • TIPSY output tables omit initial age rows corresponding to regen Delay, i.e., TIPSY Ages 0-1 in this example.   This does not apply to BatchTIPSY.

  • TIPSY Ages and Stand Ages are the same, because regen Delays are the same.

  • Total Ages are different, reflecting planting stock age.

  • Different establishment periods for each regeneration method reflect different temporal distributions (tree counts over time):

    • Planted:  the establishment period is 1-yr, i.e., planting of 1-yr-old stock occurs after the regen Delay (TIPSY Age=2 here).

    • Natural and Clumped:  The establishment period is 11-yrs in this example.  This can range 5-11yrs depending on species and density.  Seeds begin germinating after the regen Delay (at TIPSY Age=2 here) with more germinating every year until the tree count peaks at the end of the establishment period (TIPSY Age = 13 here).   Interim mortality reduces total live stems at the end of the establishment period, e.g., 2383 vs. 2500, in this example. Annual germination numbers follow a bell-shaped distribution pattern.  

  • Slight differences in Height and BH Age result from adjustments to the Site index (SI) a measure of site productivity based on the top height [meters] of a stand at breast-heightage 50. It does not apply to stands in which the height growth of top height trees has been affected by treatment (fertilization, tree improvement) or stand density (repression). curve for Planting Stock Height.
