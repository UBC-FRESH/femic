Jobs: Surveys

Jobs required to:

perform silviculture surveys that assess planting survival, free-growing status etc.

Options:

User-specified: Your own local data

Default: 0.1 PD/ha district average. This is the same for all districts (provincial average).

Source:

Employment statistics reported by Forest Practices Branch (FPB)

Comments:

Most surveys occur at various times after stand establishment, but for simplicity TIPSY assumes they coincide with the year of establishment (i.e. at time of planting, or germination if stand is of natural origin). Regeneration delay Natural stands: years from disturbance (fire, logging) until the first successful seed year. Plantations: years from disturbance (fire, logging) until planting precedes surveys, if applicable.
