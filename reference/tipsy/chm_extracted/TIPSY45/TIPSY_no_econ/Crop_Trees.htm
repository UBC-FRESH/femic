CropTrees vs. PrimeTrees

Crop Trees

Crop Tree statistics reported in TIPSY's Custom Tables refer to a specified number of trees/ha greater than 12.5cm DBH.   TIPSY offers two options:  a) 250 trees/ha or b) a user-specified value (trees/ha).   Crop tree statistics are available for: Quadratic Mean Diameter (DBHg), Basal Area (BA) total cross-sectional area (ob) of all living trees at breast height (1.3m) at various minimum DBH limits (m2/ha), and Merchantable volume.   Tree Count (12.5+) can be used to monitor the number of Crop Trees.

Crop Tree statistics have largely replaced Prime Tree statistics in TIPSY.   Unlike Prime Trees, Crop Trees are affected by OAFs.  

Prime Trees

Prime trees are the 250 trees/ha of largest diameter (12.5+cm).   Unlike Crop Trees, Prime Trees are unaffected by OAFs.   Crop Tree statistics have largely replaced Prime Tree statistics in TIPSY.   The only remaining Prime Tree statistic is Live Crown % (LC) length of crown relative to tree height [%], only reported in TIPSY for Prime Trees, which is reported in both yield tables and custom tables.
