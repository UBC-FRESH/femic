Estimates of Juvenile Wood are available within Custom Tables and Lumber Tables in three (3) formats:

  1. Juv Wood - % Vol:  Percent (%) of total stand volume by merchantability limit.

  2. Juv Wood - Vol:  Stand volume (m3/ha) by merchantability limit.

  3. Lumber (JW Adj.):  lumber volumes by grade, with grade adjustments for juvenile wood

For more information on juvenile wood visit theTASS Wood Quality Website.

How it's Determined

The volume (m3) of juvenile wood (JW)  in the annual growth rings of each tree is determined by TASS based on transition height, i.e., the height to the JW-mature wood transition.  Transition height is generally at or just below the base of the live crown.   TASS obtains transition height  from research data (below), where available, and assumes the base of the live crown for other species.  JW volume is then accumulated for each tree and summed to per hectare values within TASS.   These JW values are included in the TIPSY database.

The following species-specific models stem from a series of large collaborative wood-quality research projects beginning in 1985 (Kellogg 1989; Jozsa, et al. 1998; Middleton et al 1995).

Transition height (HTTRANS) for lodgepole pine is determined as follows:

relHTTRANS  =

[(2.50 + 0.67 X AT – 1.85 X CL) / 21.86]0.5

 

and

 HTTRANS  =

(relHTTRANS + 1.0) X HTCB                     if relHTTRANS < 0

 =

(relHTTRANS  X (HTT – HTCB) + HTCB        if relHTTRANS > 0

Where:

 

relHTTRANS =

transition height relative to crown base

  HTTRANS =

absolute transition height (m)

 

 AT =

tree age at base

CL =

crown ratio squared = (HTCB / HTT)2

 HTCB =

height to crown base (m)

 HTT =

total tree height (m)
