CWD: Periodic Recruitment Table

The CWD Periodic Recruitment Table reports the number, or volume, per hectare of previously standing dead trees that have fallen to the ground since the previous age step. That is, the two down dead trees (or 0.001 m³) reported at age 5 in the examples below is the amount that became coarse woody debris between ages 0 and 5. Similarly if we look under the DBH Class 5 for age 20 we see that 21 trees (or 0.099 m³) became CWD between the ages of 15 and 20.

The DBH classes are 5 (0 to 10 cm), 15 (11 to 20 cm), 25 (21 to 30 cm), ..., 55 (51 to 60 cm), and 65+ (>=61 cm).
