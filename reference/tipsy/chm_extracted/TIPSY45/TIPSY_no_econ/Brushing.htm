Brushing Effects

NOTE: Brushing treatments and their yield effects are not incorporated in TIPSY.

The growth of seedlings may be reduced, or lost altogether, if competition from other vegetation for sunlight, soil moisture, or nutrients is too great. Brushing removes the competing vegetation allowing the seedlings to reach "free growing" stage. A site may require more than one brushing. In addition, brushing is considered to be a part of "basic silviculture" along with site preparation and planting, and not part of "incremental silviculture".

TIPSY does not offer an option for brushing because it reports the potential yield for a particular species, establishment density and treatment. No allowance is made for brush competition and other factors which can impede growth and productivity. Consequently, you have to assume that brush competition is controlled on those sites where it is a potential problem. Supporting information is not required unless analyses are requested via FAN$IER or "Jobs" modules. If so, default brushing in one module automatically activates the other.

As an alternative, incorporate the effect of brush on stand yield indirectly using Operational Adjustment Factors if the impact is known, or if you want to test various scenarios as part of a sensitivity analysis .
