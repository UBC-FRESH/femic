Jobs: Other

Jobs required to:

perform tasks other than those specified in TIPSY.

Options:

User-specified: Your own local data

Default: 0 PD/ha at TIPSY age 0.

Comments:

TIPSY allows you to enter jobs that occur in any year before final harvest.

The age at which the jobs occur and the description of the jobs will then appear as Other Treatment Costs in the Silviculture Costs dialog and the resulting economics table, as well as the Silvicultural Events table.

Likewise, if you enter the cost, year and description of the treatment in the Silviculture Costs dialog, the same information will appear in the Jobs dialog box and table.
