Windthrow: Example

We want to remove 60% of the area of an existing 70-year old cutblock of coastal Douglas-fir (site index 30) located in the North Island Timberland (NIT, Sayward/Tsitika/Eve River).  The cutblock prescription includes the retention of the remaining 40% of the stand to meet VR objectives.  The retained area is aggregated into groups with an average size of 3000m2 and with a top height of 31.4m at the time of harvest.  If we expect the retained area to be reduced due to windthrow, TIPSY will calculate the windthrow probability using the default mean values for the North Island Timberland (NIT).  We assumed that the windthrow damage occurs before the harvested portion of the cutblock is replanted.

Given these inputs, TIPSY predicts that the crown cover retained is reduced from 40 to 37.8%, the edge from 483 to 465 m/ha, and the VRAFs at age 100 from 0.46 to 0.49 after windthrow losses are calculated.  TIPSY predicted that windthrow loss would increase the planting area by 5%, which would increase the regenerated yields at age 100 by 6% (or 22m3).
