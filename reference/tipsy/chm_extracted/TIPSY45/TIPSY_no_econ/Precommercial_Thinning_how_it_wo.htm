Pre-commercial Thinning in TIPSY

Pre-commercial thinning (PCT) is applied through the Stand Specifications dialogue box.

Residual density is selected within the Residual Density dialogue box.   TIPSY sets upper and lower limits for residual density, taking mortality death or destruction of forest trees as a result of competition, disease, insect damage, drought, wind, fire and other factors (excluding harvesting) into consideration and the minimum post-PCT density available in TIPSY (69 trees/ha).

The timing of PCT is fixed within TIPSY at a height of 4 metres in the interior and 6 metres on the coast.  For multiple species stands, this means PCT may be applied at different ages, due to height growth differences.  Species composition has no affect on PCT timing.   However, TIPSY will not display PCT pre-post data in Yield Tables for multiple species.

How are trees selected for removal/retention?

TIPSY's PCT database is produced by TASS.  TASS's PCT routine tries to mimic an experienced operator traversing the stand in a series of 5m wide strips. The selection procedure is as follows:

  1. Divide the stand into 5m strips

  2. Select the tree of largest DBH within the first 5 m of the first strip

  3. Remove all trees within a fixed radius of the selected tree (e.g. 2.0m for a residual density of 625 trees/ha).

  4. Select the next largest tree in the same 5m square cell, and remove all trees within the same fixed radius from this tree

  5. Continue until no more trees are removed or retained in the cell

  6. Proceed to the second 5m square cell in the strip and repeat the process

  7. Continue until the entire stand has been treated

  8. Compare the actual with the requested residual density

  9. Increase or decrease the radius of the surround (2.0m +/-) and repeat the thinning`

  10. Continue adjusting the radius until the target residual density is achieved
