Percent Composition

Percent  Composition (%) represents the proportion of the overall initial stand density (stem/ha) assigned to each species.   The Percent Composition dialog box appears when additional species are selected; the total must always equal 100%.

ATTENTION:  TIPSY's multi-species yields do not reflect the full range of species dynamics associated with intimate species mixtures.  This is because TIPSY's database only contains single-species yields produced by TASS II.    See Pro-rating Multiple Species for more detail.   Note, a new version of TASS, TASS III, is now available which  simulates mixed-species yields with species dynamics.
