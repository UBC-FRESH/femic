Custom OAFs: Development

Custom OAFs provide a structure that allows subject specialists (e.g., entomologists, pathologists, etc) to define Response models consisting of a sequence of OAFs over a range of ages or heights. TIPSY interpolates intermediate values.   OAFs for three intensity (severity) levels, i.e., low, medium and high, can be defined for up to 5 ages or heights.

OAF values for each severity level always represent the impact on Merch. Volume (12.5+).

Note that Agent 1 impacts depend on age (time), whereas Agent 2 impacts are related to height (tree size).

OAFs for Agent 1 are specific to Douglas-fir (IDF) growing in the ICH BEC Zone only.   To restrict OAF application to one species, it must be implemented through Species Specifications.    However, there is no way for users to restrict OAF application to specific BEC Zone.   However, this feature can be added by TIPSY development staff.
