Defaults by Species

How/why it is applied

The Planting Stock Height dialogue box is accessed via a button on the Species Specification dialogue box.  Stock height is only applied when Planted has been selected as the regeneration method in Stand Specifications.

How/why it is applied

Planting Stock Height is only used to adjust the stand's height-age (site index) curve; it has no affect on the ages reported in TIPSY's various outputs.  Height-age curves used by TIPSY were developed from stands of natural origin.  Planting Stock Height is use to modified them slightly for use in planted stands, because the height growth of nursery-grown seedlings is normally faster than that of natural seedlings in the field.  E.g., naturally regenerated pine (site index 20) normally takes two years to reach 15 cm, the average height of one-year-old nursery stock.

To account for this difference, site curves are applied to planted stands beginning at the specified stock height rather than a height of zero.  I.e., the height-age curve is shifted to the age corresponding to the height of the planting stock. This  'age shift' allows TIPSY to report age since planting, even though age is internally referenced to the height-age curve of natural stands. This age shift gives planted trees a head start, and the effect lasts the entire rotation.  At age 40 (years since planting), the planted pine in the example above would have the same site height as a natural stand at age 41 (years since germination in the field).

If TIPSY yield curves are used as input to forest-level models (e.g., timber supply models) that already incorporate stock height, set TIPSY stock height to 0, overriding the default.  The forest-level model should then apply its own stock height and recalculate mean annual increment.

NOTE:  tree improvement effect (genetic worth) is applied separately in TIPSY.
