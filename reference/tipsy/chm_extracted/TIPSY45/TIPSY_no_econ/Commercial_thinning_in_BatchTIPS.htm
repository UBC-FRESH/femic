Commercial thinning, Batch

You can commercial thin stands of coastal Douglas-fir and interior lodgepole pine only. Three treatment heights are available for each species. Intermediate heights and ages may be available in the future.

The heights shown in the following table were chosen to bracket a reasonable range of treatment opportunities for each species in terms of merchantable volume, both removed and left standing, on all sites.
