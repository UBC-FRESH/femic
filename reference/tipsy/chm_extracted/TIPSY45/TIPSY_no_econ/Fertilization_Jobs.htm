Jobs: Fertilization

Jobs required to:

promote tree growth on sites deficient in one or more soil nutrients.

Options:

User-specified: Your own local data

Default: 0.1 PD/ha district average. This is the same for all districts (provincial average)

Source:

Employment statistics reported by Forest Practices Branch (FPB)

and Mike Stone (1992).

Comments:

  • Fertilizer is applied at the ages specified in the Stand Specifications dialog box.

  • OAFs don't affect the number of jobs created by fertilization.
