Years to breast height (YTBH)

Years to breast height (YTBH) is the number of years from germination to a height of 1.3 m (i.e. breast height).

TIPSY uses Site index (SI) a measure of site productivity based on the top height [meters] of a stand at breast-heightage 50. It does not apply to stands in which the height growth of top height trees has been affected by treatment (fertilization, tree improvement) or stand density (repression). curves that relate Top Height to Breast height (BH) Age.   Each species-specific site index curve comes with a YTBH function that predicts YTBH as a function of site index.  TIPSY uses YTBH to convert BH Age to Total Age.   

Current BC site curves were developed with data from natural stands.  Consequently, the associated YTBH functions reflect the average growth rates of natural seedlings.  Planted nursery-stock normally exhibits fewer YTBH.   There currently is no way to adjust for this in TIPSY, but efforts are underway.
