Variable Retention: User's Perspective

TIPSY processes the information you have entered into its variable retention dialog, determines the variable retention adjustment factor (VRAF), and then applies the VRAF in a 3-step process as follows:

1. The first step in reducing yields is similar in effect to TIPSY’s OAF 1 This step removes the retained area(s) from timber production altogether, according to the percentage crown cover retained. It reduces the initial density at the time of planting, like an OAF 1.

2. The next step takes effect as crowns grow and the retained group expands into harvested areas. About half of the regenerated trees that are overtopped by the retained portion of the stand will die and move into TIPSY’s mortality table, thus reducing yield as well. ‘About half’ is our best estimate, and TIPSY moves these trees to the mortality table in the first height step, rather than over time. Prime tree statistics and lumber recovery factors aren’t affected.

3. What about the remaining 50 percent of the trees that are affected by the crowns of the retained stand? Partial shading causes them to grow more slowly than the balance of the regenerated stand, reducing yield without affecting mortality, i.e. the number of live trees is unaffected by this reduction.

As noted above, these 3 adjustments combine to make up the VRAF. TIPSY reports the VRAF at age 100. You can also view the impact of the VRAF in the custom table. The VRAF reported in the custom table, however, is calculated by its affect on merch. volume 12.5+. This means that you will see a VRAF of 1.00 in each step of your table until there is merch. volume 12.5+.

Variable retention has no affect on that part of the regenerated stand growing beyond the shadows of the retained stand. See Assumptions used in the VR Model for more information on shading.

It's important to note that the retained stand is not included in the harvest.

For mixed species stands, the VRAF is calculated for each species, then the resulting single-species yields are pro-rated according to the species composition of the stand. If you choose to calculate edge length using the formula option, TIPSY uses the leading species for those calculations.

We have developed VR coefficients for only four species in TIPSY. If you use variable retention for any other species, the program automatically makes substitutions. See Table of VR species substitutions.
