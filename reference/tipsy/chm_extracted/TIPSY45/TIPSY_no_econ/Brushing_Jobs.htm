Function:

JOBS = f (Area, Slope, #Trees treated, Regional adjustment)

JOBS = 2.86 - (0.0267 * A) + (0.016 * S) + (0.0000407 * T) + R

where:

JOBS = Average number of person days per hectare

A = Total area of stand treated in hectares (default 50 ha)

S = Average slope % of the stand (default 10%)

T = Average number of trees per hectare on the site to be treated (provided by TIPSY)

R = Region-specific adjustment as follows:

Comments:

  • You can specify the brushing age. Stands are brushed to help them reach the free-growing stage. Consequently, TIPSY applies an upper height limit of 5 m for treatment (i.e., the brushing window ranges from 0 years to the age associated with a height of 5 m). Later brushing or conifer release is not a JOBS option in TIPSY.

  • Employment is related to the number of trees in the stand. TIPSY assumes that all trees are released at a fixed amount of labour per tree.

  • TIPSY also assumes that brushing competition has no impact on growth. Consequently, the trees must be freed of brush competition or killed via OAFs. The impact of the combined OAFs will be incorporated automatically when TIPSY provides the "post-OAF" number of trees to be treated.

See also OAFs and Brushing Costs.
