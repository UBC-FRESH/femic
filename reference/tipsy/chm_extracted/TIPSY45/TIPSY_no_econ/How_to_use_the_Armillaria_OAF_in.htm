Armillaria OAF: Interactive and Batch

TIPSY includes a Custom OAF for Armillaria root rot specific to interior Douglas-fir in the ICH (Interior Cedar-Hemlock BEC Zone).   I.e., TIPSY will only apply this OAF to interior Douglas-fir in the ICH.

Within interactive TIPSY

The  Custom OAF labelled Armillaria in ICH only appears when:

  1. the ICH BEC Zone has been selected in the Project Title and Stand Geography dialogue box and

  2. interior Douglas-fir has been added in the Species Specification dialogue box.

Within BatchTIPSY

In the Input Variables dialogue box, there is a check box labelled Armillaria in ICH.   To apply this OAF,  the datafile must include the following for each analysis unit containing infected interior Douglas-fir within the ICH:

  1. a BEC Zone column containing ICH

  2. a severity column containing L for low severity, M for medium or H for high.  All other characters are ignored.

For more on forest-level applications, see Stearns-Smith et al 2004.

What is Armillaria root rot?

Armillaria root rot, also known as the honey mushroom or shoestring root rot, is caused by several species in the genus Armillaria (e.g., agaricales, tricholomataceae, etc.). The one that most often attacks conifers in British Columbia is A. ostoyae.  It colonizes stumps from which it can attack the surrounding trees, and may act in conjunction with insects and other pathogens. It is a chronic and endemic problem plaguing the forests of southern British Columbia especially in the southern interior.
