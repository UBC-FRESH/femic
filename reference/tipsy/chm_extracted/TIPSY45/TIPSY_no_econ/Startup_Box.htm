Start-up Box

The Welcome to TIPSY start-up box displays when TIPSY first opens.  Its sole purpose is to help novice users move past the initial blank screen.  It can be toggled off permanently by checking the "do no show" box, or by selecting No Start-up Box from the Preferences menu.

NOTE:  The first two icons on the Toolbar provide the same functions as the two options in the start-up box.
