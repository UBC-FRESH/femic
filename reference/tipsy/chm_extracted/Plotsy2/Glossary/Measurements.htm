Measurements

Currently all measurements are in 1/1000” inch.  A future upgrade may include mm, for now conversions will have to be done outside of the program.  Although thousandths of an inch sounds like a weird unit to measure in, it is simple when using imperial paper.  Most paper we print on is still based on imperial sizes, like Letter (8 1/2” x 11”), Legal (8 1/2” x 14”) and Tabloid (11” x 17”).

1” = 1000

1/2” = 500

1/4” = 250

1/8” = 125

1/16” = 63

1/32” = 31

 

To convert mm to thousandths of an inch, multiply mm by 39.37

10mm (1cm) = 394

9mm = 354

8mm = 315

7mm = 276

6mm = 236

5mm = 197

4mm = 157

3mm = 118

2mm = 79

1mm = 39
