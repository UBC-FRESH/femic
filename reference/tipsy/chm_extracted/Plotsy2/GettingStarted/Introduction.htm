Introduction

Plotsy was developed to graph data created with Ministry of Forests and Range (MOFR) growth and yield software applications. It replaces Plot32, which was used with TIPSY, TASS and SiteTools.

 

Plotsy was designed to be a flexible graphing application for displaying growth and yield data and model trends, for distribution to clients in the Ministry, industry and consulting.
