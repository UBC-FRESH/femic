Years to Breast Height

Years to Breast Height (Y2BH) refers to the number of years from germination to breast height.   Site Index models are normally published with simple Y2BH models, which are also included in SiteTools and Sindex.   Y2BH models are used to convert between breast height age and total age.   Where they exist, Site Index models that include Juvenile Height Growth models can provide more accurate estimates of Y2BH and early height-growth.
