Ministry Recommended Curves

The Ministry Recommended Curves in SiteTools are the standard (default) curves used in Ministry growth and yield, inventory, silviculture, and planning applications, including timber supply (AAC) analyses.  SiteTools also includes other curves for some species, mainly for comparison purposes.

The default version of the Species Assignment Table utilizes only Ministry Recommended Curves.  Therefore, SiteTools only uses Ministry Recommended Curves, unless curve assignments have been changed in the Species Assignment Table.
