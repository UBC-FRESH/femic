Site Index Models

Conventional Site Index models are also called height/age models, because they are formulated to predict site height from breast height age and site index.   SiteTools (Sindex) can also invert these equations to predict any one of these three variables from the other two.

SiteTools (Sindex) contains one or more site index models for 24 of BC'c most common (commercial) tree species.   Each of these 24 species has one site index model designated as ministry recommended for BC applications.    Notes describing each model are also accessible in SiteTools (Sindex).

The Species Assignment Table enables users to assign specific Site Index and Growth Intercept models to various species codes, including codes for species that lack models for BC.

Site Index models are usually published with simple Years to Breast Height (Y2BH) models used to convert between breast height age and total age.  Where they exist, Site Index models that include Juvenile Height Growth models can provide more accurate estimates of Y2BH and early height-growth.

SiteTools (Sindex) can also pair site index models with Growth Intercept models (1-50 yrs BH) for improved accuracy at young ages.
