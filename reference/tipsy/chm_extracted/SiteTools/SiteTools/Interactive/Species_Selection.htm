Common Species the default option, designed to meet the needs of most users, i.e., applying Ministry Recommended curves to 24 common BC-native tree species.   Citations and Notes are provided for the SI and GI curves applied, as governed by the Species Assignment Table.  Manual curve selection is not available.

All Species provides access to all species codes (136 default) in the Species Assignment Table, both native and exotic.   Citations and Notes are provided for the SI and GI curves applied, as governed by the Species Assignment Table.  Manual curve selection is not available.

Advanced enables manual curve selection within the 24 common (commercial) native species with Site Index curves in SiteTools.
