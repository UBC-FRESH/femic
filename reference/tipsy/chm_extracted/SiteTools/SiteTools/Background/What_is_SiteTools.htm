What's in SiteTools?

Overview

SiteTools is a user-friendly Windows interface for making Site index (SI) A measure of site productivity based on site height [meters] of a stand at breast-height age 50. calculations.  It contains and integrates a wide range of site index-related models available for many commercial tree species in British Columbia (BC), including the models recommended by the BC Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development
 for use in silviculture and forest planning.

Site Index is one of the most common measures of forest Site Productivity The capacity of land to grow trees governed by climatic and edaphic characteristics. in use around the world today.   In BC, Site Index is defined as the Site Height [m] height of the largest dbh tree of the target species in a 0.01 ha plot. The tree must be healthy, vigorous, dominant or co-dominant, undamaged and unsuppressed. attained by a stand at 50 years Breast Height age.  Site index is a key attribute within forest inventories and silviculture surveys, and a key input and growth driver within BC's principle suite of growth and yield (GY) models:  TASS, TIPSY, and VDYP.   

Interactive SiteTools can make individual site index calculations in Calculator mode or generate site index tables in Table mode.   Batch mode automates site index calculations within large data files.   See Getting Started.

What's in SiteTools?

SiteTools incorporates and integrates four types of site index-related models:

  • site index (SI, height-age) models

  • growth intercept (GI) models

  • juvenile height growth (JHG) models

  • species conversion models

SiteTools obtains this suite of functions from the SINDEX.DLL file.  This same .DLL is also incorporated in TASS, TIPSY, and VDYP to facilitate consistent SI calculations across ministry GY applications.   SINDEX.DLL is also available for download by itself, for programmers wishing to incorporate the same suite of site index functions in other applications.
