User Support

The Stand Development and Modelling Group, within the
Forest Analysis and Inventory Branch of the
British Columbia Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development
conducts and coordinates SiteTools research and development activities, provides user support, and advises the Minister, Executive, and staff on growth and yield issues.

Please help us assist you by documenting the following steps before contacting user support:

  1. Briefly document what you are attempting to accomplish and the problems you encounter.

  2. If appropriate, also document your steps and attach a copy of your input files (if any) so that we can reproduce the problem.

  3. Email this documentation to the appropriate user support contact below.

We also welcome suggestions for improving SiteTools in the future.
