Calculation Settings, Batch

In Batch mode, Calculation Settings are found in the middle of the left side of the main screen.

Calculation Settings in Batch operate identically to those in both interactive modes, with one exception:   Individual Calculation Settings are superseded by the presence of the corresponding variable(s) within an input file, i.e., location, regeneration.

Location Codes accepted in data files

  • c, coast, or coastal (case insensitive),  otherwise assumed interior.

Regeneration Codes accepted in data files

  • n or natural (case insensitive),  otherwise assumed planted.
