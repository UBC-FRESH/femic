## Source and domain

- Source: Tompalski et al. (2025), national RSYC curves,
  https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpaf067, CC BY 4.0.
- Secondary method source: Tompalski et al. (2024),
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2024.121894.
- Pilot: Montane Cordillera / Cariboo Basin; Douglas-fir (`PSEU.MEN`) and lodgepole pine
  (`PINU.CON`); age 1–300.
- The RSYC R package is GPL-3. This change uses published numeric parameters and the
  published equation; no R package code is copied.

## Equation and carbon conversion

``AGB = b1 * exp(-b4 * age) * (1 - exp(-b2 * age)) ** b3``

Carbon:

``carbon_t_c_ha = AGB_t_ha * carbon_fraction``

The pilot declares **0.47**, from IPCC (2006) Guidelines, Vol. 4, Ch. 4, Table 4.3.
Boundary: aboveground live biomass only; excludes belowground biomass, dead wood, litter,
soil carbon, and harvested-product pools.

## Pilot comparison

| Species | Age | AGB t/ha | Volume m3/ha | Carbon tC/ha |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PSEU.MEN | 20 | 59.2 | 125.2 | 27.8 |
| PSEU.MEN | 50 | 103.4 | 224.0 | 48.6 |
| PSEU.MEN | 100 | 118.4 | 255.3 | 55.7 |
| PINU.CON | 20 | 43.8 | 87.5 | 20.6 |
| PINU.CON | 50 | 70.8 | 146.9 | 33.3 |
| PINU.CON | 100 | 81.6 | 170.4 | 38.4 |

Biomass and volume are independently selectable response families with different units.
They are not additive. The ratio is a comparison, not a conversion factor.

## CLI

```bash
femic efi biomass-curve config/efi/biomass/rsyc_cariboo_pilot.json \
  --species PSEU.MEN --age 50 --json
```

## Limits

- One ecodistrict and two species; not a BC-wide biomass map.
- Published RSYC curves, not local FEMIC validation.
- The curve may decline at old ages; do not impose monotonicity beyond the source.
- The carbon fraction is a planning value, not a species-specific measurement.
- No P115 carbon package, CBM runtime, tag, or release change.
